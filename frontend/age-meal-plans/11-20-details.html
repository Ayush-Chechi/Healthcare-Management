Complete 7-Day Meal Plan for Teens (11-20 Years)

Day 1: Growth Focus

Breakfast: Protein smoothie with banana, spinach, and protein powder
Lunch: Chicken and avocado wrap with whole grain tortilla
Snack: Greek yogurt with honey and nuts
Dinner: Lean steak with roasted vegetables and quinoa
Post-workout: Chocolate milk or protein shake

Day 2: Brain Fuel

Breakfast: Oatmeal with walnuts and blueberries
Lunch: Salmon salad with mixed greens
Snack: Apple with almond butter
Dinner: Turkey meatballs with whole wheat pasta
Evening: Herbal tea and dark chocolate
