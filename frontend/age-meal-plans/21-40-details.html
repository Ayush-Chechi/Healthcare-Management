Complete 7-Day Meal Plan for Adults (21-40 Years)

Day 1: Energy & Productivity

Breakfast: Avocado toast with poached eggs and spinach
Lunch: Grilled chicken salad with mixed greens and vinaigrette
Snack: Handful of almonds and an orange
Dinner: Baked salmon with asparagus and wild rice
Hydration: 2-3 liters of water throughout the day

Day 2: Metabolism Boost

Breakfast: Greek yogurt with chia seeds and berries
Lunch: Lentil soup with whole grain bread
Snack: Protein bar or hard-boiled eggs
Dinner: Stir-fried tofu with vegetables and brown rice
Evening: Herbal tea for digestion
