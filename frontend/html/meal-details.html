General Nutrition Guidelines

Fruits & Vegetables

At least 5 portions daily. Rich in vitamins, minerals, and fiber for overall health.

Proteins

Include lean meats, fish, eggs, beans, and nuts. Essential for muscle maintenance.

Whole Grains

Choose brown rice, oats, and whole wheat. Provide sustained energy and fiber.

Hydration

Drink 8-10 glasses of water daily. Essential for all bodily functions.

Dairy Products

Include milk, yogurt, and cheese. Important source of calcium and protein.

Healthy Fats

Olive oil, avocados, nuts. Support heart health and nutrient absorption.
