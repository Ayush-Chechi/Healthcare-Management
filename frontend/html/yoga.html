🌿 Benefits of Yoga

Stress Relief

Yoga calms the nervous system and reduces anxiety, bringing a sense of peace and relaxation.

Improves Health

Boosts immunity, enhances blood circulation, and promotes overall well-being.

Strength & Flexibility

Strengthens muscles, improves flexibility, and enhances body posture.

🧘 Types of Yoga Practices

Hatha Yoga

Gentle practice focusing on basic poses and breathing techniques.

Vinyasa Flow

Dynamic practice linking breath with movement, building energy and focus.

Yin Yoga

Slow-paced practice with longer holds for deep relaxation and flexibility.
